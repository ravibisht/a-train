Put a green-screen clip here as atrain.mp4 and run:  bash ~/vpn-split/app/build.sh
The app keys the green to its navy and plays it muted, looping, behind the dashboard.
For "A-Train Status Greenscreen" by The Mining Meteor (YouTube: 3nxXwfkTkuU), download it yourself;
the creator asks for credit, which the app's footer shows. make-dmg.sh bundles the clip if present.
